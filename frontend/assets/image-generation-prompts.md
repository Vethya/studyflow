# StudyFlow Character Generation Brief

## Required visual system
Create a cohesive premium 2D character system for a university study-planning SaaS.
Style: modern editorial vector illustration, clean geometric forms, expressive but restrained,
soft rounded shapes, subtle paper-like depth, no gradients, no glossy 3D, no anime, no clip-art.
Palette: zinc neutrals with indigo #6366F1, teal #14B8A6, amber #F59E0B, rose #F43F5E.
Use consistent line weight, lighting, proportions, eye style, and rendering across every asset.
Transparent background. No words, letters, watermarks, logos, books with readable text, or UI.

## Avatar contact sheet prompt
Generate one 4×3 contact sheet containing 12 distinct university-student profile avatars.
All characters use the exact same illustration system and shoulder-up composition, facing forward,
centered in circular-safe framing. Include diverse skin tones, genders and gender-neutral looks,
hair textures and styles, glasses, hijab, headphones, and one graduation-cap option.
Hair must have natural full hairlines and believable volume—no receding or disconnected shapes.
Expressions are friendly, confident, and calm. Clothing is simple contemporary campus wear.
Each tile must have equal dimensions, consistent head scale, margins, and soft solid backgrounds.
Avoid caricature, exaggerated features, childish faces, photorealism, and corporate stock-photo style.
Deliver at high resolution; every avatar must remain recognizable at 32px.

## Flo mascot master prompt
Design “Flo,” StudyFlow’s original scholar-owl mascot, as a premium modern SaaS character.
Flo has one invariant silhouette, facial anatomy, indigo plumage, cream face/chest, amber beak/feet,
and a small charcoal graduation-cap detail. Friendly to university students, not childish or plush-like.
Create a 4×3 expression/action sheet with identical proportions and rendering in every tile:
1 neutral/welcome, 2 happy/task complete, 3 proud/weekly goal, 4 focused/study session,
5 encouraging/behind schedule, 6 concerned/deadline near, 7 urgent/overload,
8 sad/missed session, 9 thoughtful/schedule revision, 10 celebrating/streak,
11 resting/break reminder, 12 empty-state/no tasks. Use pose and expression—not text—to communicate.
Keep urgency supportive rather than frightening. Transparent background, equal tile sizes.

## Production rules
Generate contact sheets first for art-direction approval. After approval, regenerate each selected
asset individually at 1024×1024 PNG with transparency. Preserve the approved design exactly.
Also request SVG only if the generator produces clean editable vectors; otherwise keep PNG/WebP.
Do not import any asset into Figma until the full set passes consistency review.
